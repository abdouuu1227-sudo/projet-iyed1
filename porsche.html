Porsche is a prestigious German car manufacturer known for
producing high-performance sports cars, luxury vehicles, and SUVs.
Founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche, the brand has built a strong
reputation for combining speed, precision engineering, and elegant
design. Iconic models like the Porsche 911 showcase its legacy of
innovation and racing excellence, making Porsche one of the most
respected names in the automotive industry.
